# Zombie Night: Rescue

Android 2D pixel-art action game for **Zombie Night: Rescue**.

## Visual direction

The game follows the supplied reference direction: detailed pixel-art characters, warm cinematic interiors, dark blue HUD panels, city-night lighting, animated combat, weapons, zombies and bosses.

## Story

You begin at home on an ordinary Monday morning. The city suddenly collapses into a zombie outbreak. After surviving the streets, the intelligent boss **El Cerebro** kidnaps your girlfriend and takes her to a prison. Your objective is to survive, collect weapons, defeat the infected and rescue her.

## Gameplay

- Touch controls for Android landscape mode.
- Movement, attack and interact controls.
- Fists, bat, pistol, bottle and sword support.
- Common, fast and heavy zombies.
- Boss encounter with **El Bruto / El Cerebro** progression.
- Apartment opening, city survival and prison chapter.
- Health, score, weapon HUD and story messages.

## Build

GitHub Actions builds the debug APK automatically on pushes to `main`.

APK output: `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`.
